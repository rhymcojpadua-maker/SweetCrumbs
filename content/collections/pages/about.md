We started Cakelle because the best cakes rarely live where the biggest audiences are. They live in home kitchens in Quezon City, small studios in Makati, and family bakeries in Cebu. Cakelle brings those bakers into one considered marketplace.

For customers, that means discovering real bakeries, comparing real cakes, and reserving a pickup or delivery without guesswork. For bakers, it means a storefront, order tools, inventory, and payments that let them focus on the work they love.

Every bakery on Cakelle is reviewed before it opens. Every order is tied to one bakery, so nothing is lost between storefronts. And every payment is verified by PayMongo before an order moves forward.
