We collect the information needed to process an order: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and order details. Payment card information is handled by Xendit and is never stored on SweetCrumbs servers.

Bakeries receive the information required to fulfill your order. We use order data to provide support, prevent fraud, and improve the marketplace. We never sell your personal information.

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your account information by writing to hello@cakelle.ph.
